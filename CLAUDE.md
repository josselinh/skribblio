# Skribblio Tools — Project Guidelines

Skribblio Tools lets users build custom word lists (alone or collaboratively) and export them for Skribbl.io games. Visitors can browse public content without an account: the Explorer (`/explore`) and the pages of public groups and collections, with the Skribbl.io export of a group (exporting a collection needs an account). Everything else (creating, contributing, voting, favorites, sharing, the dashboard, user pages) requires a registered, logged-in user with a verified email address (the email is used for notifications). Visitors never see the names of the users.

## Architecture

- The front-end is a **Vue 3 + TypeScript single-page application** living in `resources/js`, built by Vite. It is not Inertia and not Blade: do not add Blade pages or Inertia.
- Laravel is a **JSON API** consumed by the SPA. Laravel serves a single view, `resources/views/app.blade.php`, for every front-end path (see `routes/web.php`).
- `routes/web.php` declares named SPA routes (`dashboard`, `login`, `password.reset`, `verification.notice`) because Laravel and Fortify generate redirects and email links to those names. Any other front-end path is handled by `Route::fallback`, which returns a JSON 404 for `api/*` or JSON requests.
- **Authentication**: Sanctum in SPA mode (session cookie + CSRF, `statefulApi()` in `bootstrap/app.php`) with **Fortify headless** (`'views' => false`). Fortify features enabled: registration, password reset, email verification, profile update, password update. Two-factor auth and passkeys are intentionally disabled.
- Fortify endpoints live at the root (`/login`, `/register`, `/logout`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password`, `/email/verification-notification`); the app's API lives under `/api`.
- After authentication, users land on `/dashboard` (Fortify `home` is `/dashboard`).
- **Installable web app**: `public/manifest.webmanifest` (standalone, `start_url` `/`), icons in `public/icons` (`icon.svg`, also the favicon, and `icon-maskable.svg` are the sources of the PNGs, rendered with `rsvg-convert`; `public/favicon.ico` too), declared in `app.blade.php`. No service worker: the app needs the API anyway. The `theme-color` meta follows the header's background, synced by `@/lib/theme` whenever the theme or the style changes.
- **Maintenance** (`php artisan down`): page loads get `resources/views/errors/503.blade.php`, self-contained (inline CSS, no build assets), in the browser's language (`Accept-Language`, as the maintenance middleware runs before `SetLocale`; texts in `lang/*/maintenance.php`) and the device's theme; an SPA already open gets JSON 503s, and the axios interceptor of `app.ts` then shows the `common.maintenance` toast once instead of leaving only the generic errors. Tests use the `array` maintenance driver (the `file` one would also put the development server down).

## Domain model

- **Word** (`words`): `name`, `word_group_id`, `created_by`, `updated_by` (who renamed it last, `Word::renameBy()`, `updater` in the resource for logged-in users; null until renamed, and once that user is deleted). Belongs to exactly one group; the name is unique within its group, at most 32 characters (`Word::NAME_MAX_LENGTH`) and contains no comma, because Skribbl.io uses commas as separators. A word has no visibility of its own: it is visible to whoever can see its group. **Votes** (`word_votes`, `Word::voters()`, pivot `value` +1/−1, composite primary key): every logged-in user who can see the group gives a word a thumb up or down (`WordVote` enum), its author included, whether the group is open to contributions or not. Votes are an indicator for contributors; the export can leave out the words scored too low (see the Skribbl.io export), and votes are not copied by duplication.
- **WordGroup** (`word_groups`): `name`, `visibility`, `accepts_contributions` (default false), `contributions_members_only` (default false), `created_by`. Has many words; belongs to many group collections through the `groups_collections` pivot (keys `group_collection_id`, `word_group_id`, plus `added_by`: who added the group, null once that user is deleted; `GroupCollectionWordGroup` pivot model with `addedBy()`, shown in the footer of the cards of the collection page on one line as "Ajouté par X, il y a 3 jours" (`added_by`, logged-in users only, and `added_at`, the pivot's `created_at`)). The model is named `GroupCollection`, not `Collection`, to avoid confusion with Laravel's collection classes.
- **GroupCollection** (`group_collections`): `name`, `visibility`, `accepts_contributions` (default false), `contributions_members_only` (default false), `created_by`. Belongs to many word groups through `groups_collections`. A collection may be empty: it can be created without any group, lose all of them (removed, or deleted) and be copied with none (its page says so).
- **Content language** (`language`, `App\Enums\Locale` fr|en, `HasLanguage` trait: cast and `inLanguage(?Locale)` scope; `Locale` and `CONTENT_LOCALES` from `@/lib/contentLanguage` in the SPA), independent of the interface language, which offers more languages: the language of a group's words and of a collection's groups. The language is chosen on creation and **never changes** (`language` is prohibited by the update requests; `LanguageField` only appears in the creation forms, `LanguageBadge` everywhere else). **A collection never mixes languages**: every group it contains is in its language (creation takes the language given, otherwise its first group's, and refuses other groups; attaching a group in another language is a 422). Users choose one preferred content language (`users.content_locale`, set at registration to their interface language, English when the content is not offered in it, e.g. Chinese: `InterfaceLocale::contentLocale()`, `contentLocaleOf()` in the SPA), the default of new groups and of the Explorer; copies keep the language and are named in it (" (copy)").
- **Favorites**: users favorite groups (`favorite_word_groups`) and collections (`favorite_group_collections`), with composite primary keys.
- `visibility` is the `App\Enums\Visibility` enum (`Public`: every logged-in user can see; `Private`: only the creator). Groups and collections share the `App\Models\Concerns\HasVisibility` trait (`creator()`, `isCreatedBy()`, `isVisibleTo()`, `visibleTo($user)` scope).
- Authorization lives in `app/Policies`:
    - Only the creator can update or delete a group or a collection.
    - **Contributions** ("Ouvert aux contributions", `AcceptsContributions` trait: `acceptsContributionFrom()`, `allowsChangingContributionOf()`): the creator always manages the content. While a group is open, anyone who can see it adds words and edits or deletes their own; while a collection is open, anyone who can see it adds groups and removes the ones they added. Once closed, only the creator changes the content, and earlier contributions are kept (reopening gives contributors their rights back). Name, visibility, the option and deletion stay with the creator.
    - **Members-only contributions** (`contributions_members_only`, `restrictsContributionsToMembers()`, `@/lib/contributions` in the SPA): on a public item open to contributions, only the creator and its direct members (accepted through the share link, see below) contribute; everyone still sees it and votes. The flag has no effect on a private item or a closed one. Revoking a member removes their rights; their contributions are kept. Making the item private turns its members into viewers.
    - **Type of sharing** (`App\Enums\ShareType`, `share_type` column of the members tables, partial by default): the creator picks it when accepting a request (`share_type` of the accept endpoint, `ShareTypeField` in the confirmation) and changes it at any time (`PATCH /api/{word-groups|group-collections}/{id}/members/{userId}`, a selector per member in `AccessManagement`, confirmed in `ConfirmDialog`; the member gets the optional `ShareTypeChanged` email). A partial member manages their own contributions; a full member also renames and deletes every word of the group, or removes every group of the collection (`allowsChangingEveryContribution()`, `manageContributions` policy, `can.manage_contributions`: the edit pages then list everything, not "Vos mots"/"Vos groupes"). It only applies while the item is open to contributions and still shared (private, or public open to its members only: `Shareable::sharesFullyWith()`); name, visibility, options, sharing and deletion stay with the creator, and a full member of a collection still only sees the groups visible to them.
    - A word can be edited or deleted by the creator of its group, or by its author while the group is open.
    - Whoever can add groups to a collection can attach any group they can see, including other users' public groups and their own private groups (hidden from the other viewers).
    - Favoriting requires being able to see the item.
- A public collection may contain private groups. Other users must not see those groups: filter with `$groupCollection->wordGroups()->visibleTo($user)`. **Nothing ever tells a viewer that a collection contains groups hidden from them** (no count, no notice, no access request from the collection), in its page, its export or its duplication.
- Deletions cascade: deleting a user deletes everything they created, except the words they added to other people's groups, which stay without author (`words.created_by` null, shown as "Compte supprimé"); deleting a group deletes its words and detaches it from collections; deleting a collection keeps its groups.
- Factories have `public()`, `private()`, `openToContributions()`, `openToMembersOnly()` (public, open, members only), `closedToContributions()` and `createdBy($user)` states. `GroupCollectionFactory` attaches a group of the same creator and language automatically when none was attached (detach it for an empty collection).
- **Sharing private items** (`Shareable` trait, used with `HasVisibility` and `AcceptsContributions`), and public items open to their members only (the link then asks to contribute: `hasAccess()`, `purpose: contribution` in `ShareInvitationResource` and `AccessRequestResource`, `isForContribution()` on requests and adapted emails):
    - The creator gets a secret share link (`share_token`, generated on demand, regenerable to invalidate old links, never serialized). Opening `/share/groups/{token}` or `/share/collections/{token}` lets a user request access; the creator is emailed (`AccessRequested`), accepts or refuses (`AccessRequestDecided` email), and can revoke access later.
    - Requests live in `word_group_access_requests` / `group_collection_access_requests` (one row per item and requester, `AccessRequestStatus`: pending, accepted, refused; asking again after a refusal reopens it). Accepted requesters become members (`word_group_members` / `group_collection_members`).
    - Visibility = public, or creator, or member **while the item is open to contributions** (a private item closed to contributions is only visible to its creator; its members, kept, get their access back when it reopens; `Shareable::isShareable()`: open, and private or members only, gates the `share` policy, the share link (404 otherwise) and accepting requests (422 otherwise); "Partagés avec moi" only lists the memberships the user can see). A member of a private group can see it and add words. **Being a member of a collection gives no access to the private groups it contains**: like any viewer, the member sees only the groups they can see by themselves, without learning that others exist.
    - Access requests sent from a collection (a removed feature) carry `via_group_collection_id`: the existing ones are still shown to the creator and must never reveal the group's name to the requester (not listed in "shared with me", refusal email without the name).
    - Policies: `share` (creator, private item or public item open to its members only; `ShareButton` and `AccessManagement` are shown on `can.share`, with a `purpose` prop) `manageAccess` (creator) and `leave` (members only).

## Back-end conventions

- **Every primary key is a UUID**: models use `HasUuids`, migrations use `$table->uuid('id')->primary()` and `foreignUuid()` / `uuidMorphs()`. Never use `id()` or `foreignId()`.
- New business endpoints go inside the `auth:sanctum` + `verified` group of `routes/api.php`, in controllers under `app/Http/Controllers/Api`, validated by Form Requests (authorizing through the policy). The public read endpoints (both searches, `GET` of a group, of its words and of a collection, the export of a group; the export of a collection stays in the authenticated group) live in a second group, declared after it so that `word-groups/search` is not taken for a group id, with the `AuthenticateIfVerified` middleware (authenticates through Sanctum when possible, treats an unverified user as a guest) and `throttle:public-api` (`AppServiceProvider`, per user or IP). The policies and scopes they use accept a null user (`view(?User)`; `visibleTo(null)` keeps public items only; `withIsFavorite`, `loadIsFavorite` and `withVotes` add nothing for a guest). Resources compute `can` with `Gate::allows()` (all false for guests) and leave out every `creator` for guests. Guests get a 422 on `author=me|others` and `sort=popular|largest` (`SearchRequest::MEMBERS_ONLY_SORTS`) and on `sort=author|score` (words, `ListWordsRequest::MEMBERS_ONLY_SORTS`). Current endpoints:
    - `GET /api/word-groups`: the current user's own groups (12 per page, most recently updated first, with `creator` and `words_count` like the search, so that dashboard cards match the Explorer's). `POST /api/word-groups` creates one; `GET`/`PATCH`/`DELETE /api/word-groups/{id}` show/update/delete one (`DELETE /api/group-collections/{id}` likewise; creator only, 404 when the user cannot see the item). The edit pages of a group and of a collection show, in their last section, a "Supprimer" button (`DeleteButton`, `can.delete`, confirmed in `ConfirmDialog`), then go back to the dashboard.
    - Favorites: `PUT`/`DELETE /api/word-groups/{id}/favorite` and `/api/group-collections/{id}/favorite` (idempotent; adding requires seeing the item, removing is always allowed). `GET /api/favorites/word-groups` and `GET /api/favorites/group-collections` list them (12 per page, most recently favorited first), only the items the user can still see: a favorite on an item that became private or whose access was revoked is kept in the database but left out, and shows again if access comes back.
    - Group and collection resources expose `is_favorite` when computed: use the `withIsFavorite($user)` scope on lists and `loadIsFavorite($user)` on a single model (`Favoritable` trait); never compute it per item.
    - Sharing: `GET`/`POST /api/{word-groups|group-collections}/{id}/share-link` (get / regenerate), `GET .../access` (pending requests and members), `DELETE .../members/{userId}`, `DELETE .../membership` (the current user, a member, leaves the item: `leave` policy, `can.leave`, `Shareable::leave()`; like a revocation, they can ask again, and their favorites are kept, hidden from the favorites lists while they cannot see the item); `GET`/`POST /api/share/{word-groups|group-collections}/{token}[/access-request]` (invitation status: accessible, pending, refused, none); `GET /api/access-requests/received`, `POST /api/access-requests/{kind}/{id}/accept|refuse`; `GET /api/shared/word-groups|group-collections` (the items the user was granted access to; pending requests are not listed).
    - `GET /api/search/word-groups` and `GET /api/search/group-collections` (`q`, `language=fr|en` (every language when left out), `author=all|me|others`, `sort=recent|popular|largest|name`, `direction=asc|desc` (defaulting to `SearchSort::defaultDirection()`), `per_page` ≤ 24, `page`): paginated search, by name only, over what the user can see; names starting with `q` first, then the `sort` (`App\Enums\SearchSort`: last update by default, number of favorites, number of words / groups, name; the most recent, popular or largest first and alphabetical unless `direction` says otherwise), then most recently updated; results include `creator` and `words_count` / `word_groups_count`. Name search and author filtering are the `nameContains`, `orderByNameRelevance`, `orderBySearchSort` and `createdByAuthor` scopes of the `HasSearchableName` trait.
    - `GET /api/word-groups/search?q=` (optional `language`, used to offer only the groups of a collection's language): up to 10 groups visible to the user whose name contains `q` (own groups first), with their `creator`; used by the group picker.
    - `GET /api/word-groups/{id}/collection-targets` (`q`, `group_collection_id`; anyone who can see the group): up to 10 collections the user can add the group to (`acceptingContributionsFrom($user)` scope of `AcceptsContributions`, matching `acceptsContributionFrom()`), in the group's language, each with `contains_word_group`. With `group_collection_id`, only that one if still a target; with `q`, a name search (own first); otherwise suggestions, the user's favorites (most recently favorited first) or, when none is a target, their own collections, told by `meta.suggestions` (`favorites`|`own`).
    - `GET /api/group-collections`: the current user's own collections (12 per page, with `creator` and `word_groups_count`). `POST /api/group-collections` creates an empty one (in the user's content language unless `language` is given).
    - `GET`/`PATCH /api/group-collections/{id}`: show (with the `word_groups` visible to the user, each with its `words_count`) / update a collection. `POST /api/group-collections/{id}/word-groups` (`word_group_id`) adds a group and records `added_by`, `DELETE /api/group-collections/{id}/word-groups` (`word_group_ids`, up to 100) removes several at once, possibly all of them, all or nothing (403 when one of them cannot be removed by the user); there is no per-card "Retirer" button: on the collection's edit page, the cards the user may remove carry a checkbox (a label covering the card), and "Supprimer la sélection" is confirmed in `ConfirmDialog`. Create and update endpoints of groups and collections accept `accepts_contributions` and `contributions_members_only`. Both touch the collection's `updated_at`.
    - `GET`/`POST /api/word-groups/{id}/words`: list (20 per page, `sort=name|author|created|updated|score` and `direction=asc|desc`, `App\Enums\WordSort`: most recently added first by default, the direction defaulting to `WordSort::defaultDirection()`, authors deleted last, score = upvotes − downvotes then most upvotes; each word has `upvotes_count`, `downvotes_count`, `my_vote` from the `withVotes($user)` scope; `author=me` keeps the current user's words, 422 for guests) / add the words of a group. `POST` takes `names` (up to 500, split by the SPA from a comma- or newline-separated text): it is lenient, adds the valid words, skips those already in the group (as the database compares them, regardless of case and accents) and returns `{ data, skipped, invalid: [{ name, message }] }` (201 when a word was added, 200 otherwise); `AddWordForm` leaves the invalid ones in its textarea. `PATCH`/`DELETE /api/words/{id}` (shallow routes) rename/delete a word. `PATCH /api/word-groups/{id}/words` (`words: [{ id, name }]`) renames and `DELETE /api/word-groups/{id}/words` (`word_ids`) deletes up to 100 words of the group at once, all or nothing (403 when one of them is not allowed; a name held by another word of the group, even one renamed in the same request, is refused, errors keyed `words.N.name`). The SPA only uses these bulk endpoints. `PUT /api/words/{id}/vote` (`vote`: `up`|`down`) / `DELETE /api/words/{id}/vote` record or remove the current user's vote (idempotent, `vote` policy, return the word with its counts).
    - `POST /api/word-groups/{id}/duplicate` (`duplicate` policy: anyone who can see the group, members of a private group included): copies the group (`WordGroup::duplicateFor()`) into a new group of the current user named "… (copie)", private and closed to contributions, with every word written by them (bulk insert in a transaction); favorites, members, share link and collections are not copied, and the copy keeps no link to the original. The group page shows a "Dupliquer" button (`can.duplicate`) and opens the copy.
    - `POST /api/group-collections/{id}/duplicate` (`duplicate` policy: anyone who can see the collection): copies the collection ("… (copie)", private, closed to contributions) with a copy of each group the user can see, made by `WordGroup::duplicateFor()`, except the user's own groups, which are kept as they are; with `copy_word_groups: false` (the modal's "Copier aussi les groupes" checkbox, checked by default), the copy contains the visible groups themselves, none copied; hidden groups are left out without a word; the copy is empty when no group is visible. Everything happens in one transaction (`GroupCollection::duplicateFor()`). Both pages use `DuplicateButton` ("Dupliquer", in the actions row of the title, confirmed in a `ModalDialog` that explains what is copied); the copy name comes from the `HasCopyName` trait.
    - `GET /api/word-groups/{id}/export` and `GET /api/group-collections/{id}/export`: every word (unpaginated, sorted by name, duplicates kept, each as `{ name, score }`, score = upvotes − downvotes from the `withScore` scope) of a group, or, for a collection, its groups visible to the user (sorted by name) each with its words, so that the SPA combines the chosen groups without reloading; allowed to anyone who can see the item (visitors included for a group, logged-in users only for a collection).
- `DELETE /api/user` (`CurrentUserController`, `password` checked with `current_password`) deletes the current user's account: it logs out first (logging out saves a remember token, which would recreate a deleted user), then deletes their sessions, API tokens, password reset tokens and the user, in a transaction. The profile page has a "Supprimer mon compte" section (password asked in a `ModalDialog`), then goes to the login page with a status message. Queued notifications are marked `#[DeleteWhenMissingModels]`.
- Resources expose a `can` object (e.g. `can.update`, `can.add_word`, `can.add_word_group`, and `can.remove_from_collection` on groups listed by a collection) computed from the policies, so the SPA shows or hides actions without duplicating authorization rules.
- A private group the user cannot see answers **404, not 403** (`Response::denyAsNotFound()` in the policy; Form Requests return `Gate::inspect(...)` to keep that status).
- Adding, renaming or deleting a word touches its group's `updated_at` (`#[Touches(['wordGroup'])]` on `Word`). Only `GET /api/user` (the SPA needs it to know where to redirect) and `DELETE /api/user` are reachable by unverified users, besides the public endpoints, where they are treated as guests.
- API responses always go through Eloquent API Resources (`app/Http/Resources`), with snake_case keys and ISO 8601 dates.
- **Interface languages**: French (default, `APP_LOCALE=fr`), English and Simplified Chinese (`App\Enums\InterfaceLocale` fr|en|zh, cast of `users.locale`; `InterfaceLocale` and `LOCALES` from `@/lib/i18n` in the SPA). Chinese is offered for the interface only, never for the content. laravel-lang publishes its `zh_CN` files in `lang/zh` (alias in `config/localization.php`). The `SetLocale` middleware (web and api groups) uses the logged-in user's `users.locale`, otherwise the `X-Locale` header the SPA sends with every request (a visitor's language, also stored on the account at registration), otherwise French. `User` implements `HasLocalePreference`, so every email is sent in its recipient's language. This is the interface language only; the language of the content (words) is a separate notion. Translations come from `laravel-lang/common` (dev dependency) and are published in `lang/`; refresh them with `vendor/bin/sail artisan lang:update`. Add every new user-facing string in French, English and Chinese (`LocaleTest` checks that every interface language has the same keys as French, back-end and SPA). Messages produced by the back-end (validation messages and field names, policy denials, emails, generated names such as " (copie)") are never written in the code: they live in per-domain PHP translation files of `lang/fr`, `lang/en` and `lang/zh` (`words.php`, `word_groups.php`, `group_collections.php`, `access_requests.php`, `duplication.php`, `account.php`, `fields.php` for validation attribute names) and are read with explicit keys and placeholders (`__('words.add.max', ['max' => self::MAX_NAMES])`), never with the French text as key. Do not add them to the files generated by laravel-lang (`validation.php`, `fr.json`, `en.json`), which `lang:update` may overwrite.

## Front-end conventions

- Directory layout in `resources/js`: `pages/` (route components, `pages/auth/` for authentication, one sub-folder per domain such as `pages/word-groups/`), `components/` (reusable UI), `api/` (one module per API resource, e.g. `api/wordGroups.ts`, exposing typed functions), `stores/` (Pinia, setup syntax, for shared state such as the logged-in user), `composables/`, `router/`, `lib/` (HTTP client, formatting, labels), `types/`. Use the `@/` alias for imports.
- Authenticated pages are wrapped in `components/AppLayout.vue` (header + main container + `AppFooter`: open source licence, GitHub links, non-affiliation notice; also shown under `AuthCard` on auth pages).
- **Breadcrumb** (`@/lib/breadcrumb`, `AppBreadcrumb` at the top of `AppLayout`): it follows the path the user actually took from the dashboard or the Explorer ("Tableau de bord › Noël › Animaux de la ferme", "Explorer › Camille Martin › Métiers"), through the pages of groups, collections and users, and the edit pages, always last. The trail is kept in the history state of each entry (never in the URL), so it survives back/forward and reloads; a step keeps its query (the Explorer comes back with its filters); going back to a page of the trail cuts it there; it holds the root and the last steps, four levels at most with the current page. Arriving without a trail (link, new tab, from an account page), it starts at the dashboard, or the Explorer for visitors. Pages give their label with `useBreadcrumb(label, parent?)`; an edit page also gives its item's page (`itemCrumb`), shown when the trail does not end with it. Phones show the whole trail on one line, scrolled to its end and swiped horizontally (no scrollbar, a fade on the left while its start is hidden); larger screens truncate each step.
- **Group and collection pages are for reading, their editing has its own page.** `pages/word-groups/ShowWordGroupPage.vue` (`/groups/{id}`) shows the read-only title (`ItemHeader`, with the favorite star and, in a row at the bottom of its card (`actions` slot, passed only when it has buttons; secondary buttons styled with `@/lib/buttons`), "Gérer" (creator, `can.update`) or "Contribuer" (`can.add_word`) linking to the edit page, "Dupliquer", "Ajouter à une collection" and "Quitter"; on phones, all but "Gérer"/"Contribuer" only show their icon, the label kept for screen readers by `labelHiddenOnPhonesClass`), the pending access requests for the creator (`PendingAccessRequests`, `can.manage_access`, only when there are some, with Accept/Refuse; also on the collection page), the export (alone, so that its panel opens right under its button, sliding in), then the words (`WordListItem`, read-only; its votes, always shown, use `WordVoteButtons`, optimistic like `FavoriteButton`, clickable by every logged-in user who sees the group). `pages/word-groups/EditWordGroupPage.vue` (`/groups/{id}/edit`, route `word-groups.edit`, also opened after creating a group) has sections: "Paramètres" (creator: `NameVisibilityForm`, name, visibility and the `ContributionsField` checkbox with a "Qui peut contribuer ?" choice on public items), "Mots" (`AddWordForm`, its "Ajouter" button under the textarea on the right, then the current page of words in `WordListEditor`: a text field per editable word, saved at once with only the changed ones sent: they are highlighted with a "Rétablir" button, counted on "Enregistrer (N)" in a bar that sticks to the bottom of the screen while there are changes, and "Annuler" asks before discarding them; checkboxes and "Supprimer la sélection" confirmed in `ConfirmDialog`; the votes end each line, only displayed (`WordVoteButtons` `readonly`); under each word, like in the read-only list, `WordMeta`: "Ajouté par X, il y a 2 mois", followed once renamed by "· Modifié par Y, à l'instant", on one line scrolling horizontally when it does not fit; sorting and pagination are hidden while changes are unsaved), "Partage" (`can.share`: `ShareButton` and `AccessManagement`) and "Supprimer le groupe"; a contributor only sees "Vos mots": the form and their own words (`author=me`), not the others' they cannot change. Likewise, `pages/group-collections/ShowGroupCollectionPage.vue` (`/collections/{id}`) shows `ItemHeader` with "Gérer" (`can.update`) or "Contribuer" (`can.add_word_group`), "Dupliquer" and "Quitter" in its actions row, the export, then the group cards; `pages/group-collections/EditGroupCollectionPage.vue` (`/collections/{id}/edit`, route `group-collections.edit`, also opened after creating a collection) has "Paramètres", "Groupes" (`WordGroupSearch` adding a group, confirmed in `ConfirmDialog`, then the cards, selectable for "Supprimer la sélection"), "Partage" and "Supprimer la collection"; a contributor only sees "Vos groupes": the search and the groups they added (`added_by`, filtered in the SPA as the collection's groups are not paginated).

# Skribblio Tools — Project Guidelines

Skribblio Tools lets users build custom word lists (alone or collaboratively) and export them for Skribbl.io games. Visitors can browse public content without an account: the Explorer (`/explore`) and the pages of public groups and collections, with the Skribbl.io export of a group (exporting a collection needs an account). Everything else (creating, contributing, voting, favorites, sharing, the dashboard, user pages) requires a registered, logged-in user with a verified email address (the email is used for notifications). Visitors never see the names of the users.

## Architecture

- The front-end is a **Vue 3 + TypeScript single-page application** living in `resources/js`, built by Vite. It is not Inertia and not Blade: do not add Blade pages or Inertia.
- Laravel is a **JSON API** consumed by the SPA. Laravel serves a single view, `resources/views/app.blade.php`, for every front-end path (see `routes/web.php`).
- `routes/web.php` declares named SPA routes (`dashboard`, `login`, `password.reset`, `verification.notice`) because Laravel and Fortify generate redirects and email links to those names. Any other front-end path is handled by `Route::fallback`, which returns a JSON 404 for `api/*` or JSON requests.
- **Authentication**: Sanctum in SPA mode (session cookie + CSRF, `statefulApi()` in `bootstrap/app.php`) with **Fortify headless** (`'views' => false`). Fortify features enabled: registration, password reset, email verification, profile update, password update. Two-factor auth and passkeys are intentionally disabled.
- Fortify endpoints live at the root (`/login`, `/register`, `/logout`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password`, `/email/verification-notification`); the app's API lives under `/api`.
- After authentication, users land on `/dashboard` (Fortify `home` is `/dashboard`).

## Domain model

- **Word** (`words`): `name`, `word_group_id`, `created_by`. Belongs to exactly one group; the name is unique within its group, at most 32 characters (`Word::NAME_MAX_LENGTH`) and contains no comma, because Skribbl.io uses commas as separators. A word has no visibility of its own: it is visible to whoever can see its group. **Votes** (`word_votes`, `Word::voters()`, pivot `value` +1/−1, composite primary key): while the group is open to contributions, whoever can see it gives a word a thumb up or down (`WordVote` enum), except its author, even when only its members can add words; once closed, votes are kept and shown but nobody can vote. Votes are an indicator for contributors; the export can leave out the words scored too low (see the Skribbl.io export), and votes are not copied by duplication.
- **WordGroup** (`word_groups`): `name`, `visibility`, `accepts_contributions` (default false), `contributions_members_only` (default false), `created_by`. Has many words; belongs to many group collections through the `groups_collections` pivot (keys `group_collection_id`, `word_group_id`, plus `added_by`: who added the group, null once that user is deleted). The model is named `GroupCollection`, not `Collection`, to avoid confusion with Laravel's collection classes.
- **GroupCollection** (`group_collections`): `name`, `visibility`, `accepts_contributions` (default false), `contributions_members_only` (default false), `created_by`. Belongs to many word groups through `groups_collections`. A collection may be empty: it can be created without any group, lose all of them (removed, or deleted) and be copied with none (its page says so).
- **Content language** (`language`, `App\Enums\Locale` fr|en, `HasLanguage` trait: cast and `inLanguage(?Locale)` scope), independent of the interface language: the language of a group's words and of a collection's groups. The language is chosen on creation and **never changes** (`language` is prohibited by the update requests; `LanguageField` only appears in the creation forms, `LanguageBadge` everywhere else). **A collection never mixes languages**: every group it contains is in its language (creation takes the language given, otherwise its first group's, and refuses other groups; attaching a group in another language is a 422). Users choose one preferred content language (`users.content_locale`, set at registration to their interface language), the default of new groups and of the Explorer; copies keep the language and are named in it (" (copy)").
- **Favorites**: users favorite groups (`favorite_word_groups`) and collections (`favorite_group_collections`), with composite primary keys.
- `visibility` is the `App\Enums\Visibility` enum (`Public`: every logged-in user can see; `Private`: only the creator). Groups and collections share the `App\Models\Concerns\HasVisibility` trait (`creator()`, `isCreatedBy()`, `isVisibleTo()`, `visibleTo($user)` scope).
- Authorization lives in `app/Policies`:
    - Only the creator can update or delete a group or a collection.
    - **Contributions** ("Ouvert aux contributions", `AcceptsContributions` trait: `acceptsContributionFrom()`, `allowsChangingContributionOf()`): the creator always manages the content. While a group is open, anyone who can see it adds words and edits or deletes their own; while a collection is open, anyone who can see it adds groups and removes the ones they added. Once closed, only the creator changes the content, and earlier contributions are kept (reopening gives contributors their rights back). Name, visibility, the option and deletion stay with the creator.
    - **Members-only contributions** (`contributions_members_only`, `restrictsContributionsToMembers()`, `@/lib/contributions` in the SPA): on a public item open to contributions, only the creator and its direct members (accepted through the share link, see below) contribute; everyone still sees it and votes. The flag has no effect on a private item or a closed one. Revoking a member removes their rights; their contributions are kept. Making the item private turns its members into viewers.
    - A word can be edited or deleted by the creator of its group, or by its author while the group is open.
    - Whoever can add groups to a collection can attach any group they can see, including other users' public groups and their own private groups (hidden from the other viewers, who can request access).
    - Favoriting requires being able to see the item.
- A public collection may contain private groups. Other users must not see those groups: filter with `$groupCollection->wordGroups()->visibleTo($user)` and tell them that some groups are hidden.
- Deletions cascade: deleting a user deletes everything they created, except the words they added to other people's groups, which stay without author (`words.created_by` null, shown as "Compte supprimé"); deleting a group deletes its words and detaches it from collections; deleting a collection keeps its groups.
- Factories have `public()`, `private()`, `openToContributions()`, `openToMembersOnly()` (public, open, members only), `closedToContributions()` and `createdBy($user)` states. `GroupCollectionFactory` attaches a group of the same creator and language automatically when none was attached (detach it for an empty collection).
- **Sharing private items** (`Shareable` trait, used with `HasVisibility` and `AcceptsContributions`), and public items open to their members only (the link then asks to contribute: `hasAccess()`, `purpose: contribution` in `ShareInvitationResource` and `AccessRequestResource`, `isForContribution()` on requests and adapted emails):
    - The creator gets a secret share link (`share_token`, generated on demand, regenerable to invalidate old links, never serialized). Opening `/share/groups/{token}` or `/share/collections/{token}` lets a user request access; the creator is emailed (`AccessRequested`), accepts or refuses (`AccessRequestDecided` email), and can revoke access later.
    - Requests live in `word_group_access_requests` / `group_collection_access_requests` (one row per item and requester, `AccessRequestStatus`: pending, accepted, refused; asking again after a refusal reopens it). Accepted requesters become members (`word_group_members` / `group_collection_members`).
    - Visibility = public, or creator, or member. A member of a private group can see it and add words. **A member of a private collection also sees the private groups it contains, but only those created by the collection's creator** (consent rule: sharing a collection never exposes other people's groups; members of a public collection, who were only allowed to contribute, do not see its private groups). `WordGroup` overrides `isSharedWith()` / `orWhereSharedWith()` for this.
    - On a collection, a viewer can request access to all the groups hidden from them in one action; those requests carry `via_group_collection_id` and must never reveal the groups' names to the requester (not listed in "shared with me", refusal email without the name).
    - Policies: `share` (creator, private item or public item open to its members only; `ShareButton` and `AccessManagement` are shown on `can.share`, with a `purpose` prop) `manageAccess` (creator) and `leave` (direct members only: seeing a group through a collection membership does not make one its member).

## Back-end conventions

- **Every primary key is a UUID**: models use `HasUuids`, migrations use `$table->uuid('id')->primary()` and `foreignUuid()` / `uuidMorphs()`. Never use `id()` or `foreignId()`.
- New business endpoints go inside the `auth:sanctum` + `verified` group of `routes/api.php`, in controllers under `app/Http/Controllers/Api`, validated by Form Requests (authorizing through the policy). The public read endpoints (both searches, `GET` of a group, of its words and of a collection, the export of a group; the export of a collection stays in the authenticated group) live in a second group, declared after it so that `word-groups/search` is not taken for a group id, with the `AuthenticateIfVerified` middleware (authenticates through Sanctum when possible, treats an unverified user as a guest) and `throttle:public-api` (`AppServiceProvider`, per user or IP). The policies and scopes they use accept a null user (`view(?User)`; `visibleTo(null)` keeps public items only; `withIsFavorite`, `loadIsFavorite` and `withVotes` add nothing for a guest). Resources compute `can` with `Gate::allows()` (all false for guests) and leave out every `creator` for guests. Guests get a 422 on `author=me|others` and `sort=popular|largest` (`SearchRequest::MEMBERS_ONLY_SORTS`) and on `sort=author|score` (words, `ListWordsRequest::MEMBERS_ONLY_SORTS`). Current endpoints:
    - `GET /api/word-groups`: the current user's own groups (12 per page, most recently updated first). `POST /api/word-groups` creates one; `GET`/`PATCH`/`DELETE /api/word-groups/{id}` show/update/delete one (`DELETE /api/group-collections/{id}` likewise; creator only, 404 when the user cannot see the item). Both pages show a "Supprimer" button (`DeleteButton`, `can.delete`, confirmed in `ConfirmDialog`) after "Modifier", "Dupliquer" and "Inviter", through the `actions` slot of `EditableNameVisibility`, then go back to the dashboard.
    - Favorites: `PUT`/`DELETE /api/word-groups/{id}/favorite` and `/api/group-collections/{id}/favorite` (idempotent; adding requires seeing the item, removing is always allowed). `GET /api/favorites/word-groups` and `GET /api/favorites/group-collections` list them (12 per page, most recently favorited first), including items that became private, flagged `is_accessible: false`.
    - Group and collection resources expose `is_favorite` when computed: use the `withIsFavorite($user)` scope on lists and `loadIsFavorite($user)` on a single model (`Favoritable` trait); never compute it per item.
    - Sharing: `GET`/`POST /api/{word-groups|group-collections}/{id}/share-link` (get / regenerate), `GET .../access` (pending requests and members), `DELETE .../members/{userId}`, `DELETE .../membership` (the current user, a member, leaves the item: `leave` policy, `can.leave`, `Shareable::leave()`; like a revocation, they can ask again, and their favorites on what they can no longer see are removed, including a collection's private groups it showed them); `GET`/`POST /api/share/{word-groups|group-collections}/{token}[/access-request]` (invitation status: accessible, pending, refused, none); `GET /api/access-requests/received`, `POST /api/access-requests/{kind}/{id}/accept|refuse`; `GET /api/shared/word-groups|group-collections` (granted items and pending link requests, flagged with `access_status`); `POST /api/group-collections/{id}/hidden-word-groups/access-requests`.
    - `GET /api/search/word-groups` and `GET /api/search/group-collections` (`q`, `language=fr|en` (every language when left out), `author=all|me|others`, `sort=recent|popular|largest|name`, `per_page` ≤ 24, `page`): paginated search, by name only, over what the user can see; names starting with `q` first, then the `sort` (`App\Enums\SearchSort`: most recently updated by default, most favorited, most words / groups, alphabetical), then most recently updated; results include `creator` and `words_count` / `word_groups_count`. Name search and author filtering are the `nameContains`, `orderByNameRelevance`, `orderBySearchSort` and `createdByAuthor` scopes of the `HasSearchableName` trait.
    - `GET /api/word-groups/search?q=` (optional `language`, used to offer only the groups of a collection's language): up to 10 groups visible to the user whose name contains `q` (own groups first), with their `creator`; used by the group picker.
    - `GET /api/group-collections`: the current user's own collections (12 per page). `POST /api/group-collections` creates an empty one (in the user's content language unless `language` is given).
    - `GET`/`PATCH /api/group-collections/{id}`: show (with the `word_groups` visible to the user and `hidden_word_groups_count`) / update a collection. `POST /api/group-collections/{id}/word-groups` (`word_group_id`) adds a group and records `added_by`, `DELETE /api/group-collections/{id}/word-groups` (`word_group_ids`, up to 100) removes several at once, possibly all of them, all or nothing (403 when one of them cannot be removed by the user); the collection page has no per-card "Retirer" button: "Modifier les groupes" switches to a selection mode (the cards the user may remove get a checkbox, "Supprimer la sélection" is confirmed in `ConfirmDialog`). Create and update endpoints of groups and collections accept `accepts_contributions` and `contributions_members_only`. Both touch the collection's `updated_at`.
    - `GET`/`POST /api/word-groups/{id}/words`: list (20 per page, `sort=name|author|created|updated|score` and `direction=asc|desc`, `App\Enums\WordSort`: most recently added first by default, the direction defaulting to `WordSort::defaultDirection()`, authors deleted last, score = upvotes − downvotes then most upvotes; each word has `upvotes_count`, `downvotes_count`, `my_vote` from the `withVotes($user)` scope) / add the words of a group. `POST` takes `names` (up to 500, split by the SPA from a comma- or newline-separated text): it is lenient, adds the valid words, skips those already in the group (as the database compares them, regardless of case and accents) and returns `{ data, skipped, invalid: [{ name, message }] }` (201 when a word was added, 200 otherwise); `AddWordForm` leaves the invalid ones in its textarea. `PATCH`/`DELETE /api/words/{id}` (shallow routes) rename/delete a word. `PATCH /api/word-groups/{id}/words` (`words: [{ id, name }]`) renames and `DELETE /api/word-groups/{id}/words` (`word_ids`) deletes up to 100 words of the group at once, all or nothing (403 when one of them is not allowed; a name held by another word of the group, even one renamed in the same request, is refused, errors keyed `words.N.name`). The SPA only uses these bulk endpoints. `PUT /api/words/{id}/vote` (`vote`: `up`|`down`) / `DELETE /api/words/{id}/vote` record or remove the current user's vote (idempotent, `vote` policy, return the word with its counts).
    - `POST /api/word-groups/{id}/duplicate` (`duplicate` policy: anyone who can see the group, members of a private group included): copies the group (`WordGroup::duplicateFor()`) into a new group of the current user named "… (copie)", private and closed to contributions, with every word written by them (bulk insert in a transaction); favorites, members, share link and collections are not copied, and the copy keeps no link to the original. The group page shows a "Dupliquer" button (`can.duplicate`) and opens the copy.
    - `POST /api/group-collections/{id}/duplicate` (`duplicate` policy: anyone who can see the collection): copies the collection ("… (copie)", private, closed to contributions) with a copy of each group the user can see, made by `WordGroup::duplicateFor()`, except the user's own groups, which are kept as they are; hidden groups are not copied (the response's `hidden_word_groups_count` says how many, never which); the copy is empty when no group is visible. Everything happens in one transaction (`GroupCollection::duplicateFor()`). Both pages use `DuplicateButton` ("Dupliquer", right of "Modifier", confirmed in a `ModalDialog` that explains what is copied); the copy name comes from the `HasCopyName` trait.
    - `GET /api/word-groups/{id}/export` and `GET /api/group-collections/{id}/export`: every word (unpaginated, sorted by name, duplicates kept, each as `{ name, score }`, score = upvotes − downvotes from the `withScore` scope) of a group, or, for a collection, its groups visible to the user (sorted by name) each with its words, plus `hidden_word_groups_count` (never which groups), so that the SPA combines the chosen groups without reloading; allowed to anyone who can see the item (visitors included for a group, logged-in users only for a collection).
- `DELETE /api/user` (`CurrentUserController`, `password` checked with `current_password`) deletes the current user's account: it logs out first (logging out saves a remember token, which would recreate a deleted user), then deletes their sessions, API tokens, password reset tokens and the user, in a transaction. The profile page has a "Supprimer mon compte" section (password asked in a `ModalDialog`), then goes to the login page with a status message. Queued notifications are marked `#[DeleteWhenMissingModels]`.
- Resources expose a `can` object (e.g. `can.update`, `can.add_word`, `can.add_word_group`, and `can.remove_from_collection` on groups listed by a collection) computed from the policies, so the SPA shows or hides actions without duplicating authorization rules.
- A private group the user cannot see answers **404, not 403** (`Response::denyAsNotFound()` in the policy; Form Requests return `Gate::inspect(...)` to keep that status).
- Adding, renaming or deleting a word touches its group's `updated_at` (`#[Touches(['wordGroup'])]` on `Word`). Only `GET /api/user` (the SPA needs it to know where to redirect) and `DELETE /api/user` are reachable by unverified users, besides the public endpoints, where they are treated as guests.
- API responses always go through Eloquent API Resources (`app/Http/Resources`), with snake_case keys and ISO 8601 dates.
- **Interface languages**: French (default, `APP_LOCALE=fr`) and English (`App\Enums\Locale`). The `SetLocale` middleware (web and api groups) uses the logged-in user's `users.locale`, otherwise the `X-Locale` header the SPA sends with every request (a visitor's language, also stored on the account at registration), otherwise French. `User` implements `HasLocalePreference`, so every email is sent in its recipient's language. This is the interface language only; the language of the content (words) is a separate notion. Translations come from `laravel-lang/common` (dev dependency) and are published in `lang/`; refresh them with `vendor/bin/sail artisan lang:update`. Add every new user-facing string in French and in English (`LocaleTest` checks that both languages have the same keys, back-end and SPA). Messages produced by the back-end (validation messages and field names, policy denials, emails, generated names such as " (copie)") are never written in the code: they live in per-domain PHP translation files of `lang/fr` and `lang/en` (`words.php`, `word_groups.php`, `group_collections.php`, `access_requests.php`, `duplication.php`, `account.php`, `fields.php` for validation attribute names) and are read with explicit keys and placeholders (`__('words.add.max', ['max' => self::MAX_NAMES])`), never with the French text as key. Do not add them to the files generated by laravel-lang (`validation.php`, `fr.json`, `en.json`), which `lang:update` may overwrite.

## Front-end conventions

- Directory layout in `resources/js`: `pages/` (route components, `pages/auth/` for authentication, one sub-folder per domain such as `pages/word-groups/`), `components/` (reusable UI), `api/` (one module per API resource, e.g. `api/wordGroups.ts`, exposing typed functions), `stores/` (Pinia, setup syntax, for shared state such as the logged-in user), `composables/`, `router/`, `lib/` (HTTP client, formatting, labels), `types/`. Use the `@/` alias for imports.
- Authenticated pages are wrapped in `components/AppLayout.vue` (header + main container + `AppFooter`: open source licence, GitHub links, non-affiliation notice; also shown under `AuthCard` on auth pages).
- Editing happens in place on the detail page (e.g. `EditableNameVisibility` turns the page title of a group or collection into an inline form, including the `ContributionsField` checkbox, with a "Qui peut contribuer ?" choice on public items; `ContributionsBadge` shows "Contributions ouvertes" or "Contributions sur autorisation" on pages and cards, `WordListItem` is read-only, and "Modifier les mots" switches the current page of words to `WordListEditor` (a text field per editable word, saved at once with only the changed ones sent: they are highlighted with a "Rétablir" button, counted on "Enregistrer (N)", and "Annuler" asks before discarding them; checkboxes and "Supprimer la sélection" confirmed in `ConfirmDialog`; sorting and pagination are hidden meanwhile); its votes, always shown, use `WordVoteButtons`, optimistic like `FavoriteButton`, clickable only while the group is open) rather than on separate edit pages. Modals use `ModalDialog` (native `<dialog>`: bottom sheet on phones, centered card from `sm`); `ConfirmDialog` wraps it for confirmations (destructive by default, `tone="primary"` otherwise), e.g. accepting an access request, revoking a member, requesting access to the hidden groups of a collection and adding a group to a collection or removing the selected ones from it. The words of a group are a list rather than a table, the same at every screen width (two lines per word: the name and its votes, then author, dates and actions), sorted with `WordSortControl` (one pill per criterion and a button reversing the order; `sort`/`direction` stay in the URL, defaults left out, and sorting goes back to page 1); collections of items to open are cards.
- A horizontally scrolling container (`overflow-x-auto`) must also be `relative`: otherwise the absolutely positioned elements inside it (`sr-only` labels) escape its clipping, widen the page on phones and push fixed elements such as notifications below the screen.
- Paginated lists use Laravel's paginator (`PaginatedResponse<T>` in `types/api.ts`), keep the page in the URL query (`?page=N`, or a dedicated parameter such as `groups_page` when a page shows several lists) and render `components/PaginationNav.vue` (`page-param` prop). Load them with the `usePaginatedList(fetchPage, pageParam)` composable.
- The header (`AppLayout`) has, after the app name (linking to the dashboard, or to the Explorer for visitors), a "Tableau de bord" link (logged-in users only) and an "Explorer" link (the current section is highlighted), a search field, then `UserMenu` or, for visitors, "Se connecter" and "Créer un compte"; "Explorer" and the search field lead to `/explore` (`pages/explore/ExplorePage.vue`, route `explore`), whose filters (`q`, `type`, `lang` (fr, en or all; defaults to `preferredContentLanguage()` from `@/lib/contentLanguage`, the user's content language or the interface's for a visitor), `author`, `sort`, `page`) live in the URL. Without `q`, the page is the "Explorer" catalogue for discovering what others share: the author filter defaults to `others` (to `all` with a search); default values stay out of the URL. The dashboard invites users with no group and no favorite to explore. Results are rendered with `SearchResultsSection` (typed `item` slot). Cards show "par X · N mots" through `CardMeta` when the resource includes `creator`/counts; use `creatorLabel()` from `@/lib/creator` to display "vous" for the current user.
- Group and collection cards are built on `ItemCard`: the whole card links to the item, the favorite star (`FavoriteButton`, optimistic, reverted on failure) sits outside the link, and an item with `is_accessible: false` is greyed out and not clickable while its star stays usable. Card lists use `CardListSection` (dashboard and search).
- The dashboard shows, in order: an invitation to explore for users with no group and no favorite, received access requests (only when there are some), own groups, own collections, then, only when they have items (after the own lists so that their appearance does not push the page down), favorite groups, favorite collections, groups shared with me, collections shared with me (page parameters `favorite_groups_page`, `favorite_collections_page`, `groups_page`, `collections_page`, `shared_groups_page`, `shared_collections_page`). Pending requests in the shared lists are greyed out like inaccessible favorites.
- User pages: clicking another user's name (card "par X", word author, access requester) opens `/users/{id}` (`pages/users/UserPage.vue`), listing that user's groups and collections visible to the current user (`GET /api/users/{id}`, `/api/users/{id}/word-groups`, `/api/users/{id}/group-collections`; never the email). The current user's own page redirects to the dashboard. Display names with `UserLink` (plain text for the current user). Cards use a stretched link on the title (`ItemCard`), so other links inside a card must sit above it (`relative z-10`).
- Account: the header's `UserMenu` (initials, pseudo, ▾) opens a menu with "Mon profil" (`/profile`), "Sécurité" (`/security`), "Préférences" (`/preferences`) and "Se déconnecter"; the three pages (`pages/account/`) share the `AccountNav` tabs. The user's `name` is shown as "Pseudo" (registration and profile). The profile page uses Fortify's `PUT /user/profile-information` (pseudo, email; a new email un-verifies the account and sends a verification email) and ends with the account deletion; the security page uses `PUT /user/password` (requires `current_password`), both through the auth store. The preferences page saves each change at once (optimistic) through `PATCH /api/user/preferences` (`PreferencesController`: `locale`, `content_locale` ("Langue des listes"), also switchable by everyone from the footer (`AppFooter`, `useLocaleChoice`), `theme`, `App\Enums\Theme` system|light|dark, and `email_notifications`, a partial map of `App\Enums\EmailNotification` to booleans merged into `users.email_notifications`, every email off until the user turns it on, read with `User::wantsEmail()`). Every optional email checks it in its `via()` (`AccessRequested`, `AccessRequestDecided`, `ItemFavorited`, sent by `Favoritable::addToFavoritesOf()` to the creator once a day at most per user and item, never for one's own item); account emails are always sent. The theme drives the `dark` class on `<html>` (`@custom-variant dark` in `app.css`), applied by `@/lib/theme` and, before the first paint, by an inline script of `app.blade.php` reading the same `localStorage` key; visitors follow the system or the last theme used on the device.
- **Skribbl.io export** (the core feature): `ExportPanel` ("Exporter pour Skribbl.io" on group and collection pages) loads the export endpoint on each opening (on a collection, after letting the user uncheck some of its groups, all checked by default) and shows the comma-separated list with a copy button, word and character counters, warnings (fewer than 10 words, more than 20,000 characters) and options: exclude the words scored too low (off by default, logged-in users only: visitors see it and get the `info` toast; a threshold of −1 or lower set with − and + buttons leaves out the words whose score is at or below it, each occurrence judged before duplicates are removed), remove duplicates (case-insensitive, on by default for logged-in users; visitors see it unchecked and get the same toast), shuffle, and a random selection that fits in the character limit. Skribbl.io's limits and the list helpers live in `@/lib/skribbl`; formatting happens in the SPA, not in the API. The list itself is `WordListExport` (props `words`, `note`), used by `ExportPanel`.
- Sharing UI: `ShareButton` ("Inviter", between "Dupliquer" and "Supprimer": a `ModalDialog` with the link, copy, regenerate), `AccessManagement` (requests and members on a private item's page), `AccessRequestItem`, `LeaveButton` ("Quitter", after "Supprimer", on `can.leave`: leaving a private item goes back to the dashboard, a public one reloads the page), and `pages/share/SharePage.vue`. A logged-out user opening a share link is sent back to it after login or email verification (`@/lib/pendingRedirect`).
- Pick groups with `WordGroupSearch` (search dropdown over visible groups, optional `language` prop, emits `select`), built on the generic `ItemSearch` (`search` function prop). The collection creation form has no group field: a new collection is empty and its groups are added on its page.
- Show user-facing dates with `formatDateTime()` from `@/lib/format` and visibility labels with `visibilityLabel()` / `visibilityDescription()` from `@/lib/visibility`.
- **Translations** (`vue-i18n`, Composition API mode, set up in `@/lib/i18n`): no user-facing text is written in components or modules. Messages live in `resources/js/lang/fr/<domain>.json` and `resources/js/lang/en/<domain>.json` (one file per domain and language, registered in `lang/<locale>/index.ts`) under explicit keys, never the French text as key. The current language comes from the user's account, otherwise from the device (`localStorage` key `locale`, read before the first paint by `app.blade.php`) or the browser, English when it is neither French nor English; switch it with `setLocale()` / `useLocaleChoice()`, read it with `currentLocale()`. Use `$t('words.add.submit')` in templates, `const { t } = useI18n()` in scripts and `i18n.global.t` outside components; put HTML or components inside a message with `<i18n-t keypath="common.byUser" scope="global">` and slots. Plurals use `|` (`"{n} mot | {n} mots"`, called as `t(key, count)`), with the French rule (0 and 1 singular) or the English one (0 plural); a three-part message starts with the zero form, in both languages. Pick the group or collection variant of a message with `itemKey(kind)`. Use `intlLocale()` for `Intl` formatters and `localeCompare`, and compute translated texts in templates or `computed()` (never once in `setup`), so that they follow a change of language. The characters `@`, `|`, `{` and `}` are special in messages.
- Confirm an action (copy, save, duplication, deletion, favorite, accepting a request or revoking a member, words added) with `showToast(message, tone?)` from `@/composables/useToasts` (`tone`: `success` by default, `error`, or `info` for a neutral message), called after `router.push` when the action leads to another page: `ToastStack` (mounted in `App.vue`, a manual popover so that it shows above modals) displays it at the bottom of the screen for a few seconds. Errors that the user must fix stay next to their form or in their dialog.
- Only guest pages, which the message is the content of, still pass a status through the router `state` (`router.push({ name: 'login', state: { status } })`), read once with `consumeHistoryStatus()` from `@/lib/historyStatus` and shown with `StatusMessage`.
- Always call the server through the shared axios instance `http` from `@/lib/http` (it sends credentials and the XSRF token). Call `ensureCsrfCookie()` before guest-side state-changing auth requests.
- Every API payload has a TypeScript interface in `resources/js/types` that mirrors its API Resource (`models.ts` for resources, `api.ts` for response envelopes such as `ResourceResponse<T>`).
- Use the `useForm` composable for forms: it exposes `data`, `errors` (first Laravel validation message per field), `isProcessing`, `submit()` and `reset()`.
- Protect routes with `meta` in `router/index.ts`: `requiresVerified` (default for app pages), `requiresAuth` (logged in, verified or not), `guestOnly`, `isPublic` (open to visitors: `explore`, `word-groups.show`, `group-collections.show`, and `home` (`/`), which sends users to the dashboard and visitors to the Explorer).
- **Visitors** (`!auth.isVerified`, matching what the API treats as a guest) on public pages: actions come from `can` and disappear by themselves, cards have no star and no author; pages also hide the author filter of the Explorer and the author of words (sort included). Never repeat the links to log in and register (the header has them, remembering the page with `redirect` and `@/lib/pendingRedirect`): an item open to contributions shows, instead of its contribution form, a plain text message (`guest.contribute.*`, or `guest.verifyEmail` for an unverified user); the hidden groups of a collection are only counted, without the access request button. A locked sort stays visible but makes no request: clicking it shows a 5-second `info` toast ("Connectez-vous pour utiliser cette option.", no links since the header has them): "Populaires" and "Les plus fournis" in the Explorer, "Votes" on a group (`lockedSorts` prop of `WordSortControl`); "Exporter pour Skribbl.io" on a collection works the same way (`locked` prop of `ExportPanel`). Public pages set the tab title with `usePageTitle()`.
- The UI is written in French and English. Styling uses Tailwind CSS v4 utility classes: neutral `zinc` tones, and the `brand` accent colour (a muted slate blue, defined in `@theme` in `resources/css/app.css`) for primary buttons, links, focus rings, selections and the current navigation item; never hard-code its values. Badges carry meaning: `LanguageBadge` (FR/EN, neutral outline) on cards and pages, public in emerald, open contributions in sky, members only in violet. The UI follows the system theme: every colour class has its `dark:` counterpart next to it (`bg-white dark:bg-zinc-900`, `text-zinc-500 dark:text-zinc-400`, `text-brand-700 dark:text-brand-300`, `bg-brand-50 dark:bg-brand-950`; see existing components).
- Use `<script setup lang="ts">` with typed `defineProps<…>()` and `defineModel()`.
